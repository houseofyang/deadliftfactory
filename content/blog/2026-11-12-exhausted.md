I'm so tired... I think that's my conclusion.\
\
I just got home after a three-month film shoot. I hadn't done a big movie in a long time, and I'm starting to think that I'm over it. (Haha!) I went to the job with a two-week mission, and in the end, I was offered a job and asked to stay for the duration of the shoot. It was my dream job at first - action movie, mostly in English, mostly a female cast, and I was treated as an exeperienced professional, at first. But as things do, the project defaulted into the usual chaos and I couldn't wait to leave.\
\
There are parts of me that think that I must have gotten soft. But maybe I'm just tired and wiser. The main producer on this film is a huge movie star and while he's brilliant and charming and knows his stuff, he's got a nasty temper and wasn't afraid to let others see it. There was a lot of shouting on this set, and I don't think I'm OK with that anymore. When I got started in the late 90s, getting shouted at was very normal. It was kind of like the only feedback you'd ever get. If you weren't yelled at, it meant that you didn't fuck up. In fact, it's been a spell since I have gotten yelled at  - I do know my job  - but I'm just over this environment where people think it's normal to scream at subordinates.\
\
Also, the set was dirty, the toilets were a fucking nightmare (something I wouldn't usually care about), we didn't have A SINGLE DAY OFF in more than three months. To also be expected, the schedule was a mess, nobody had a clue as to what was happening most of the time and all the setups became whatever whoever was in charge in that minute felt like. The thing that really broke me, though - and I realize that this sounds silly to an outsider - but I was asked to do record a ton of extra dialogue on set because the most powerful producer (someone who has made SCORES of movies and who knows better) decided that ADR was too expensive. I had no proper tools, and not even a quiet fucking place to record. It was a total waste of everyone's time, experience, energy and talent.\
\
I left as soon as the English parts were done - about a week before everyone else - and went the fuck home. I know that I could have hung on until the end and done the wrap with the team, but at this point, I don't care. Somewhere along the way, I realized that I was done with all of this and the next time I'm on a shoot, if it ever happens again, I'll be a boss.\
\
Fuck this shit.
